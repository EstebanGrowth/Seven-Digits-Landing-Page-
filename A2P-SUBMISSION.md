## Campaign

**Campaign description**
```
Seven Digits is a marketing and lead-generation business for contractors in the United States. We send text messages to contractors and business owners who book a free strategy call on our website and opt in on the booking form. Non-marketing messages confirm and remind them about their scheduled consultation and follow up on their inquiry. Marketing messages, sent only to people who separately opt in to marketing texts, share service updates and offers. Messages are sent from Seven Digits only.
```

**Message flow / call to action (how users opt in)**
```
Users opt in on our website at https://seven-digits-landing-page.vercel.app/#book. To book a free strategy call, they choose a date and time in the booking calendar and enter their name, phone, and email. The booking form has two separate, optional checkboxes that are unchecked by default: one to receive non-marketing texts about their inquiry, consultation scheduling, appointment reminders, and requested follow-up, and one to receive marketing texts. Each checkbox states the brand name (Seven Digits), that message frequency may vary, that message and data rates may apply, that users can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help, that consent is not a condition of purchase, and links to our Privacy Policy (https://seven-digits-landing-page.vercel.app/privacy-policy) and Terms and Conditions (https://seven-digits-landing-page.vercel.app/terms-and-conditions). Users can book without checking either box, and we only text users who checked a box.
```

**Sample message 1 (non-marketing)**

Seven Digits: Hi {first_name}, your free strategy call is confirmed for {date} at {time} ET. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
```

**Sample message 2 (non-marketing reminder)**
```
Seven Digits: Reminder, your strategy call with us is tomorrow at {time} ET. Need to reschedule? Reply to this message. Reply STOP to opt out.
